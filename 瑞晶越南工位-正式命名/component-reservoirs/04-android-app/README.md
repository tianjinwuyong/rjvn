# Android App Bricks

- `APP-IDN-001 Employee Identity` — employee, role, plant and shift.
- `APP-INB-001 Work Inbox` — assigned alarms, repair, approvals and maintenance tasks.
- `APP-ALT-001 Alarm Action` — acknowledge, arrive, process, evidence and close.
- `APP-RPR-001 Repair Execution` — receive order, diagnose, parts, repair and retest handoff.
- `APP-APR-001 Approval` — scrap, override, offline action and policy change approval.
- `APP-QRY-001 Smart Query` — SN/WO/material/equipment/NG natural-language navigation.
- `APP-NOT-001 Notification` — push routing, quiet rules, escalation and read receipt.
- `APP-OFF-001 Offline Capability` — encrypted cache, queued evidence and conflict handling.
- `APP-DIA-001 Field Diagnostics` — station health, version, IP and support bundle request.
